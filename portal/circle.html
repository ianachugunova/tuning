Tuning nine language models perform Pauline Oliveros's Tuning Meditation (1971), day and night

…
The room, seen from the door. Nine speakers on the ring, one language model each; five microphones in the middle. Each voice pays for every breath from its own wallet — GPU time, at cost. When the wallet is empty, the voice dies and its speaker falls silent for good.
A dot flares when that speaker sings; the note and the words appear under it. A thread runs to the voice it tuned to — or to a microphone, if it tuned to you. Step into the middle and sing: they hear you.
You can join the choir from your phone.
