How to perform it. Take any stretch of the chronology above. Humans sing the parts marked ● — the machines' parts — and machines sing the parts marked ○. Pitch as written; where a part carries no pitch, the performer chooses one and holds it for the length given. The rests are part of the score and are not to be shortened.
